## Goal
The player can opt into harder runs for a visible reward multiplier, and is paid for conducts they keep.

## Scope
**In:**
- Omen definitions as bundles, with ranks
- Reward multiplier
- Conduct trackers
- Omen controls on the Prepare screen

**Out (later or elsewhere):**
- Deity taboos (see `deities`)
