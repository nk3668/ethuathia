## Summary
Classes set which weapon and armor types they use well. Races add aptitudes, such as good with bows or bad with heavy armor.

## Why
Makes class and race choices matter for gear. See `CLAUDE.md` §5.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Proficiencies and aptitudes are data on class and race defs, validated at load
- [ ] Using a non-proficient item applies a visible penalty, shown in the tooltip
- [ ] Auto-equip prefers proficient gear and the trace says why
