## Summary
A class with a costly resurrection ability that brings a fallen party member back during the run. It needs a rare reagent, bought in town (so the party comes prepared) or found in the dungeon.

## Why
Nate's idea (2026-11-24). Death is permanent, so a way to undo one, at a real price, is a strong reason to bring this class and to prepare for it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Spec reviewed by Nate before building (spec gate)
- [ ] Only during the run: once the run ends, the dead stay dead (assumed; confirm in the spec)
- [ ] The reagent is sold in town and appears rarely in the dungeon
- [ ] The resurrected member comes back weakened; the AI and rules can trigger it
- [ ] Codex, tooltip and log text
