## Summary
The baseline AI plays to the class: healers heal hurt allies, tanks hold chokepoints, ranged classes keep distance.

## Why
Without this the AI wastes class depth. See `CLAUDE.md` §5.3.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Each class has a role used by the baseline
- [ ] The trace names the role behind a decision
- [ ] Sim tests with hand-built maps cover each role
