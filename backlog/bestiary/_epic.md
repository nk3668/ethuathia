## Goal
Monsters are varied and readable: they fight by role, belong to families, and include named uniques, elites and regular mini-bosses with better loot.

## Scope
**In:**
- Monster roles and behaviors
- Monster families and floor themes
- Named uniques
- Elites and mini-bosses

**Out (later or elsewhere):**
- Monster stat numbers and balance passes (see `tools-balance`)
- Summons and pets (see `combat-items`)
