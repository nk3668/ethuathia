## Summary
Monsters have a role (brute, skirmisher, caster, support, swarm) that sets how they fight.

## Why
Readable, varied fights from a few rules. See `CLAUDE.md` §1 (Readable).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Monster defs carry a role, shown in the Codex and inspect tooltip
- [ ] Each role has a distinct behavior (brutes close in, skirmishers hit and back off, casters keep range, support buffs or heals, swarms surround)
- [ ] Sim tests with hand-built maps cover each role
