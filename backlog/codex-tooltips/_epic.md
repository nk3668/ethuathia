## Goal
Every number, glyph and name in the game explains itself on hover, and the Codex fills in as things are discovered.

## Scope
**In:**
- `Explain` tooltips from content definitions
- Stat breakdown tooltips from modifiers
- Discovery tracking
- Codex tab

**Out (later or elsewhere):**
- Writing the descriptions themselves (see `content`)
