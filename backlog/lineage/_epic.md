## Goal
Adventurers persist, and their deaths leave gravestones, heirlooms, ghosts and descendants behind.

## Scope
**In:**
- Roster with history and XP
- Gravestones, heirlooms and ghosts
- Retirement to Tavern jobs
- Descendants
- Ossuary and Tavern trees
- Graveyard tab

**Out (later or elsewhere):**
- Grave placement during floor generation (see `dungeon-gen`)
