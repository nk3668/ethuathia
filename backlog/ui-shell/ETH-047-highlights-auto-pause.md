## Summary
A feed of notable moments (boss engaged, near death, heirloom found, contract complete), with optional auto-pause on chosen kinds.

## Why
In an idle game the player shouldn't miss the big moments. See `CLAUDE.md` §1 (Readable).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Sim events are tagged as highlights by kind
- [ ] A Highlights log filter, and clicking an entry centers the map where it happened
- [ ] Options let the player pick which kinds auto-pause the game; all are off by default
